# 🎂 Kowsi's Birthday Surprise Website ✨

A heartfelt, interactive, and beautifully designed birthday surprise website crafted especially for **Kowsi**.

---

## 🌟 Features Included

1. **🎁 Magical Mystery Gift Gate**:
   - Opens with a 3D animated birthday gift box with ribbon and sparkles.
   - Bypasses browser autoplay restrictions: clicking "Open Your Birthday Surprise" starts background music playback and triggers sound effects and confetti!

2. **🔍 Radar Scanner Preloader**:
   - Playfully scans through memories and smile records before declaring: *"Match Found: Kowsi! 💖 The Birthday Queen!"*

3. **🎵 Floating Vinyl Music Player**:
   - Plays her celebration background track (`assets/music.mp3`).
   - Includes rotating vinyl disc animation, pulsing equalizer waves, and a one-click play/pause toggle.
   - Includes a floating **"Pop Confetti 🎉"** button to burst confetti anytime!

4. **👑 Hero Celebration Stage**:
   - Floating colorful balloons and sparkles.
   - Gradient typography and emotional best-friend quotes.
   - Realistic Polaroid photo frame of Kowsi with washi tape, pin, and Queen badge.
   - Quick friendship statistics counter.

5. **🎂 Interactive Cake & Candle Ceremony**:
   - Multi-tiered cake with frosting drips, strawberries, and candle flame.
   - **"Blow Candles 🌬️"**: Flame flickers and extinguishes with rising smoke and sound effects!
   - **"Cut The Cake 🍰"**: Slices the cake, triggers applause, confetti blast, and unlocks heartfelt wishes.
   - **"Light Again 🕯️"** to replay anytime.

6. **💛 6 Superpower Friendship Cards**:
   - Glowing cards highlighting her contagious smile, golden heart, strength, late-night talks, support, and humor.

7. **📸 Scrapbook Polaroid Wall & High-Res Lightbox**:
   - 12 curated Polaroid photos with handwritten captions and washi tape.
   - Interactive heart counters that pop floating emojis when clicked.
   - Fullscreen Lightbox modal with high-res zoom.

8. **🥠 The Secret Birthday Capsule Jar**:
   - An interactive glass jar with glowing stars.
   - Tap the jar to shake and reveal 8+ personalized birthday blessings, fortunes, and promises.

9. **💌 Heartfelt Birthday Letter**:
   - Aesthetic parchment letter styling with wax seal stamp and handwritten signature.

10. **✍️ Virtual Guestbook (Sticky Notes Wall)**:
    - Interactive form to leave sweet notes and memories.
    - Saves directly to browser `localStorage` and appends colorful sticky notes.

11. **🎆 Grand Finale Midnight Fireworks & Typewriter**:
    - Fullscreen starry night overlay with realistic HTML5 Canvas particle fireworks.
    - Handwriting typewriter animation that writes out an emotional birthday message line by line.
    - Big celebration finale banner and confetti burst!

## 🚀 How to Open & View the Website

### Option 1: Directly in Browser
Double-click [`index.html`](file:///c:/Users/spidy/OneDrive/Pictures/Kowsi..❤️/Happy%20Brithday/index.html) or right-click `index.html` -> **Open With** -> **Google Chrome** (or Edge/Brave/Firefox).

### Option 2: Live Local Server
Run in PowerShell:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` on your computer or phone (if on the same Wi-Fi)!

### Option 3: Send or Host Online (1-Click Free Hosting)
- **GitHub Pages**: Push this folder to a GitHub repository and enable GitHub Pages in Settings.
- **Netlify Drop**: Drag and drop this folder onto [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) to get a free live URL (e.g., `happy-birthday-kowsi.netlify.app`) to send directly on WhatsApp!
